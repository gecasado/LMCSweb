Frequently asked questions

All donations received go entirely towards covering the club's expenses: event organisation, communications, materials, venue fees and any community initiative launched by LMCS.

Your support allows us to keep being a free, inclusive and active space for the leather and fetish community in Spain.

The Leathermen Club of Spain is a legally registered non-profit association. Donations to non-profit associations are not subject to the tax deductions under Law 49/2002, which applies exclusively to foundations and associations declared of public utility.

This means that, at present, donations to LMCS are not tax-deductible. If you have questions about your specific tax situation, we recommend consulting the Spanish Tax Agency or a tax advisor.
