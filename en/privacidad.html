PRIVACY POLICY

1. Data Controller

  • Controller: Leathermen Club of Spain (LMCS).
  • Address: Calle Zurbano, n.o 41, planta 1, Madrid.
  • Contact: lmcs@lmcspain.com

2. Purpose of Processing

Personal data collected through the forms on this website (including the Typeform form for membership applications) will be processed for the following purposes:

  • Managing the LMCS membership application.
  • Maintaining the associative relationship with members.
  • Sending communications related to the club's activities and events.
  • Responding to enquiries received through the contact form.

3. Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing your data is the consent of the data subject, provided when submitting the membership application or contact form.

In the case of members, the legal basis also rests on the performance of the associative relationship governed by the club's Bylaws.

4. Recipients

Personal data will not be disclosed to third parties, except as required by law. Application forms are managed through the Typeform platform, which acts as a data processor in accordance with its own privacy policy.

5. User Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time by sending an email to lmcs@lmcspain.com:

  • Access: Find out what personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured and commonly used format.
  • Restriction: Request the restriction of processing in certain cases.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) if you consider that your rights have not been duly addressed.

6. Data Retention

Personal data will be retained for as long as the associative relationship is maintained or until erasure is requested. Once that relationship has ended, data may be retained for the legally established period to address any potential liabilities.

7. Cookies

This website may use first-party and third-party cookies (such as those generated by the Typeform embed) to ensure the correct functioning of the site and to improve the user experience.

When accessing the website, an informational banner about the use of cookies will be displayed. You can accept, reject or configure their use at any time through your browser settings.

8. Modifications

Leathermen Club of Spain reserves the right to modify this privacy policy to adapt it to legislative or jurisprudential developments. In the event of significant changes, users will be duly informed.
